What is the Six Stars Index?

The Six Stars Index is a single number between 0 and 100 that measures how much attention GTA 6 is receiving right now. It is built from two live signals and refreshed every fifteen minutes. A reading near 100 means the game is at the peak of everything we are able to observe; a reading near 0 means the conversation has gone quiet.

It exists because neither signal means much on its own. YouTube views climb for days at a time. Twitch spikes in minutes and collapses just as fast. The Index puts both on one scale so you can tell, at a glance, whether the wave is still building.

How it is calculated

Attention50%
Live audience50%

Attention is the total views across the GTA 6 videos we track on YouTube. Live audience is the number of people watching GTA 6 on Twitch. Each is scored 0-100 on a log scale, then the two are averaged. Attention runs from 10 million views to 3 billion; live audience from 50 viewers to one million. Below those floors a signal scores zero, and only launch-night volume reaches 100.

A straight line would have pinned Twitch at zero all week and YouTube at 100 the moment the pre-loads landed. On a log scale a few hundred viewers still registers, and 100 is still reserved for the real thing. The arithmetic is on this page — if a number looks wrong, you can check it.

Data sources

YouTube Data API v3 for video statistics and search. Twitch Helix for live viewers and stream counts. All figures are fetched automatically and published as open JSON.

GTA 6 headlines are still collected from Google News RSS and published in the same JSON, but they are not part of the score.

Rankings on the Starboard are ordered by views per hour since publish, not total views — so a video from a channel with 40,000 subscribers can outrank a channel with four million.

When does GTA 6 release?

GTA 6 unlocks on 18 November 2026 at 16:30 IST (11:00 UTC). That is 6:00 AM in New York, 3:00 AM in Los Angeles, midday in Berlin, and already midnight on the 19th in New Zealand. The US — the biggest market for the game — sleeps through the first six hours, which makes that stretch the quietest window of the launch, and usually the best time to publish.

Why the leaderboard sorts by velocity

Sorting GTA 6 videos by total views returns the same five channels every day, because their back catalogue is enormous. Views per hour measures what is happening now. It is the difference between a list of the famous and a list of the current.
