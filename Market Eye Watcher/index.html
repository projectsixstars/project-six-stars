The Index

One number from 0 to 100 for how much attention GTA 6 is getting right now. Half of it is the views across every GTA 6 video we track on YouTube; half is the number of people watching GTA 6 on Twitch. Both are scored on a log scale, so a quiet week still reads clearly and launch night still has somewhere to go.

The Starboard

The top 20 GTA 6 videos on YouTube, sorted by views per hour instead of lifetime views. The same board for live Twitch streams and for clips from the last seven days. Every row links straight to the source and shows the channel, subs, age and current speed.

The Countdown

GTA 6 unlocks on 18 November 2026 at 16:30 IST, which is 11:00 UTC. The clock is written in your own timezone, so you know exactly when the window opens and when to have the upload ready.

Why views per hour

Total views show you who is famous. Views per hour show you what is working.

Sort any GTA 6 search by total views and the same five channels come back every day, because their back catalogue is enormous. That tells you nothing about what to make this afternoon. A video with 40,000 subscribers can outrank a channel with four million when it is the one actually being watched right now.

That is the whole point of the board: speed, not size. It is the difference between watching the market and reading its history.

The maths, in full

Live audience50%

Attention runs from 10 million tracked views to 3 billion. Live audience runs from 50 Twitch viewers to one million. Below those floors a signal scores zero; only launch-night volume reaches 100.

Each signal is scored 0–100 on a log scale, then the two are averaged. A straight line would have pinned Twitch at zero all week and YouTube at 100 the moment the pre-loads landed. On a log scale a few hundred viewers still registers, and 100 is still reserved for the real thing.

The arithmetic is on the tool page, next to the number. If it looks wrong, you can check it.

Where the data comes from
YouTube
YouTube Data API v3 for video statistics and search. Every video links back to YouTube; no third-party footage is hosted here.
Twitch
Twitch Helix for live viewers, stream counts and top clips.
Headlines
GTA 6 headlines are collected from Google News RSS and published in the same JSON, but they are not part of the score.
Open data
Every reading is written to current.json every fifteen minutes and kept in a public history file.
