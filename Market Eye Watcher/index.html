How often does it update?

Every fifteen minutes, which is as fast as the YouTube and Twitch APIs allow. Nothing on the page is cached longer than that.

Where does the data come from?

YouTube Data API v3 for video statistics and search, Twitch Helix for live viewers, stream counts and clips. No scraping, no third-party footage, no copyrighted material on this site.
